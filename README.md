## Selected Case Studies

Every project is built on an end-to-end PM framework:
`Problem → Users → Research → Insight → Opportunity → Prioritization → Solution → Prototype → Metrics → Experiment → Result`

| # | Project | Domain | Status | Key Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | [**Industrial AI Product — Haber**](https://kartik-mankar-portfolio.vercel.app/case-study-haber.html) | Industrial IoT / B2B SaaS | **Shipped · Real Product** | Led 100+ on-site customer discovery sessions, authored PRDs for *Kaiznn* and *Mt. Fuji*, validated ML dosing algorithms cutting chemical consumption by 15–20% and costs by 12–15% across 10+ plants. |
| **02** | [**Credit Risk Command Center**](https://kartik-mankar-portfolio.vercel.app/case-study-credit-risk.html) | FinTech / Risk Operations | **Concept Project** | Unified underwriting terminal consolidating bureau data, bank statements, and ML risk scoring — reducing decision synthesis time from 2–4 hours to <45 minutes. |
| **03** | [**FinTech Loan Funnel Optimization**](https://kartik-mankar-portfolio.vercel.app/case-study-fintech.html) | Consumer FinTech / Growth | **Concept Project** | Replaced manual bank statement uploads with RBI Account Aggregator (AA) 3-tap integration, projecting a 20–25% lift in application completion. |
| **04** | [**LaunchGuard AI**](https://kartik-mankar-portfolio.vercel.app/case-study-launchguard.html) | AI Product / Dev Tooling | **Concept Project** | Multi-agent AI governance platform auditing PRDs for edge cases, tracking cross-functional readiness checklists, and projecting a 30% reduction in preventable launch failures. |
| **05** | [**Food Delivery Product Teardown**](https://kartik-mankar-portfolio.vercel.app/case-study-food-delivery.html) | Consumer Tech / Engagement | **Teardown Exercise** | Teardown of Zomato's engagement loop, proposing an AI Weekly Meal Planner and habit streak mechanics to convert mid-tier users (4–8 orders/mo) into power users without margin-diluting discounts. |

## Technical Stack & Design System

* **Architecture:** Semantic HTML5, CSS3 Modern Glassmorphism, Vanilla JavaScript
* **3D Background:** Interactive Three.js particle canvas with dynamic cursor repulsion
